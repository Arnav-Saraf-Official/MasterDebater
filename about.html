About The Project

MasterDebater

The ultimate companion for structured debate. Built for precision, performance, and completely offline use.

Our Mission

MasterDebater was built to solve a simple problem: managing structured debates shouldn't be harder than the debate itself. We provide the tools you need to research, organize, and execute flawless arguments without relying on scattered notes or clunky spreadsheets.

Core Principles

  • Precision: Every feature is designed with exactness in mind. From timer management to argument structuring, we leave no room for error.
  • Performance: Built to be fast, reliable, and completely offline-capable. Your debate prep is available whenever you need it, instantly.
  • Innovation: Constantly iterating to provide debaters with modern tools that adapt to the evolving landscape of competitive debate.

Meet the Creator

MasterDebater is developed and maintained by Arnav Saraf. Driven by a passion for AI models and his experience with debate, he created MasterDebater to help new members get started with debate quickly.

We believe that the best tools are built collaboratively. If you'd like to explore how MasterDebater works under the hood or contribute to its development, you can view the source code and documentation on GitHub. Please read the lisence before viewing or contributing to the project.
